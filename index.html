Common questions

How does AfterBuy read my receipts?

AI extracts the product, seller, purchase date and invoice number. You review and confirm everything before it is saved.

Will I be reminded before deadlines expire?

Yes. Once a return or warranty date is saved, AfterBuy sends reminders ahead of it.

Can AfterBuy email customer support for me?

It drafts the email from your invoice details and your description of the issue. You can edit it before sending.
